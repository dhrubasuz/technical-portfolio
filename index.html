02 / Working approach

Clear requirements.
Careful delivery.

Useful software depends on more than the interface. I connect the business context, data rules and technical work, then follow the change through testing and handover.

01 — Understand

Define the problem

Identify the users, the operational need, the data and the limits. Agree what a successful change should do.

02 — Design

Make the rules visible

Map fields and system handoffs. Define validation, exceptions, access dependencies and acceptance criteria.

03 — Build & test

Check the behaviour

Work in controlled stages. Review normal inputs, invalid records and failure paths, with clear evidence of the result.

04 — Handover

Support the next person

Explain how the solution runs, what it assumes and where it stops. Keep operating guidance close to the work.

AI & productivity

I use AI to accelerate workflow analysis, automation design and documentation. I check outputs against source information and business requirements, then test the resulting workflows.
